Install and help

For the alpha version. Cannot find your problem? Write to pannolato.indoor@gmail.com.

Install

The alpha is not signed yet. Your computer asks you to confirm the first time you open the app. This is normal. You do it once.

macOS (Apple Silicon, M1 or newer)

  • Open the dmg. Drag Pannolato to Applications.
  • Open Applications. Right-click Pannolato. Choose Open. Choose Open again in the dialog.
  • If macOS says the app "cannot be opened" or is "damaged", open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and choose Open Anyway.
  • Still blocked? Open Terminal and run xattr -cr /Applications/Pannolato.app. Then open the app again.
  • macOS asks for Bluetooth access on first start. Choose Allow.

Windows (64-bit)

  • Run the exe.
  • If a blue "Windows protected your PC" window appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • If your browser blocks the download, choose Keep in the browser's download list.
  • Your antivirus can check an unknown file for a few seconds. Wait for it to finish.

Access code

  • Enter your email in the Try the alpha section of the home page. We send the code to it.
  • No email after a few minutes? Check the spam folder. Check that you typed the address right.
  • The app asks for the code once. Copy the code without spaces.
  • The code needs an internet connection the first time you enter it.
  • Still stuck? Write to pannolato.indoor@gmail.com with the email you used.

The trainer does not connect

Do these steps in order.

  • Wake the trainer. Turn the pedals a few turns. Look for the Bluetooth light.
  • Close other apps. Most trainers accept one control connection. Close Zwift, TrainerRoad, the maker's app (Wahoo, Tacx, Garmin) and any phone that is connected.
  • Check Bluetooth. Turn it on in your computer. Turn it off and on again if the list stays empty.
  • Allow Bluetooth for Pannolato. On macOS: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Bluetooth, and switch Pannolato on. On Windows: Settings, Bluetooth & devices, and switch Bluetooth on.
  • Pick the right entry. Choose the entry named after your trainer, not a heart-rate strap or a power meter.
  • Check the trainer type. The trainer must support Bluetooth FTMS, or Tacx FE-C over Bluetooth. A trainer with ANT+ only does not work. Use an ANT+ to Bluetooth adapter or the trainer's Bluetooth mode.
  • Move closer. Keep the computer within 3 metres of the trainer. A USB hub or a metal frame between them can block the signal.
  • Restart. Close Pannolato. Unplug the trainer for 10 seconds. Plug it in again. Open Pannolato.

The trainer connects but feels wrong

  • Power is wrong or jumps. Warm up the trainer for 10 minutes. Then run a spin-down in the Check the trainer page.
  • ERG does not hold the watts. Open the Check the trainer page. If it says the trainer does not accept a target power, ERG workouts cannot work with this trainer.
  • ERG feels very hard at low cadence. This is normal in ERG mode. Keep a steady cadence. Lower the target or use a lighter gear.
  • The connection drops during a ride. Close other Bluetooth apps. Keep the computer close. Do not put it behind a screen or a metal cabinet.

The heart-rate strap does not connect

  • Wet the strap electrodes. Wear the strap. A dry strap does not send.
  • Close other apps that use the strap (Garmin Connect, Zwift, a phone). Most straps accept one connection.
  • Polar: close the Polar Beat or Flow app on your phone, or turn Bluetooth off there.
  • Garmin watch: press and hold UP, open Controls, and turn on Broadcast Heart Rate. Do this before you connect.
  • Apple Watch: use a third-party app that sends heart rate as a Bluetooth sensor.
  • Wrist sensors react slowly. Expect a delay of some seconds. A chest strap is better.

During a ride

  • The world looks slow or jumpy. Close other heavy apps. Plug in a laptop. Use a lower quality setting if the app offers it.
  • No sound. Check the computer volume and the output device.
  • Group ride: I cannot join. Check your internet connection. Check the 6-character room code. Group rides need internet. Solo rides do not.

Strava and Garmin

  • Strava does not connect. The Strava API needs a Strava subscription. Without one, choose Upload by hand after the ride. Pannolato saves the FIT file and opens the Strava upload page. Drop the file there.
  • Garmin Connect. There is nothing to connect. After a ride, choose Send to Garmin Connect. Drop the saved FIT file on the import page.
  • Ride not saved. Rides shorter than one minute are not saved.

Your data

  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Pannolato
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Pannolato
  • There is no account and no cloud copy. Deleting this folder removes all your rides.
