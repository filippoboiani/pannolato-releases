The name

What is a pannolato?

In Italian cycling slang, a pannolato is an amateur rider, often not so young any more, who rides with pride in loud, colourful kit.

Many hear the pannolino, the nappy, in the word: a joke about padded cycling shorts.

Riders tease them with love. There is an Instagram page full of their outfits, and in Milan there is even a slow group ride, the Pannolata, where the only rule is to dress badly.

We make fun of the pannolati, but we are all a bit pannolati ourselves. They ride every Sunday, in any weather, and never miss the coffee stop. They are the passion for the sport and the heart of the cycling community. This app is for them.

How to spot a pannolato

  • A jersey louder than the traffic
  • Out at 8 every Sunday, rain or shine
  • A coffee stop on every ride
  • Knows every climb by its name
  • Kit that does not match, worn with pride
Train

Workouts that set the watts for you

Connect a smart trainer and a heart-rate strap over Bluetooth. Pannolato sets the resistance for each step in ERG mode, so you only pedal.

  • 70 library workouts, from recovery rides to VO2max and sprints
  • Search by what you want: “short hard session”, “45 min endurance”, “climbing”
  • Build your own workout, or upload one as JSON
  • A heart-rate cap warns you when you ride too hard
  • Change the target by ±5 W from the keyboard at any time
Plan

A training plan from six answers

Give Pannolato your goal, the weeks you have, the days you ride, the minutes per ride, your level and a start date. It builds a plan of 4, 6, 8 or 12 weeks from the workout library.

  • The Today page opens on the workout of the day
  • Busy day? Make it a rest day, or push the plan by one day
  • The plan page shows your progress, the week you are in and the next session
  • Or import a plan that ChatGPT writes from our prompt
Ride

Everything you need on one screen

Power, heart rate, cadence and speed sit at the top. The workout steps sit on the left. The mini-map, the climb ahead and your laps sit on the right. Il Pannolato comments from the corner.

  • Speed comes from your power and the gradient of the road
  • The trainer makes the hills feel like hills
  • Switch between rider view and a top view of the map
Routes

16 routes, each with a map preview

Pick a loop before you ride. The route picker shows its distance and its climb, and draws it on the map.

  • Flat, hilly and mountain loops, from 4.9 km to 58.1 km
  • The Bologna ring roads, the Riviera, the Po delta, San Luca, the Futa pass, Sestola

World

Bologna and Emilia, town by town

Start under the arcades of Bologna and climb to San Luca. Ride through Modena, Ferrara, Imola and Rimini, follow the Adriatic coast, or climb into the Apennines.

New light, water and road detail

The world now has shade in its corners, haze in its valleys, and trees and water that move.

  • Baked ambient occlusion and soft shadows
  • Height fog, and one colour grade for the whole picture
  • Trees and grass sway in the wind; rivers and canals ripple
  • Guardrails on tight bends, edge posts, and worn paint on the road

Things happen on the road

A tractor pulls out. A dog gives chase. Fans cheer at the top of a climb. And now and then, a driver passes far too close.

Imola

The Imola circuit, built on surveyed track data

Ride a lap of the Autodromo: 4.9 km and 19 turns that follow the real line and the real heights. The track has its kerbs, gravel, grandstands and pit buildings.

  • Tamburello, Tosa, Piratella, Acque Minerali, Variante Alta, Rivazza
  • A lap timer shows this lap and keeps your best lap
  • No cars on the track, and all riders lap the same way

One lap from the route picker

Autodromo di Imola is one of the 16 routes: 4.9 km with 54 m of climb, ridden anticlockwise. In a free ride, turn off at Imola to reach the circuit.

Riders

A peloton on every solo ride

On a solo ride, 8 to 16 other riders share the road. Each one holds a steady power. They pass you, you pass them, and they form small groups.

  • Sit on a wheel: drafting cuts your air drag by up to 12 %
  • Your rider follows your effort: aero on the flat, out of the saddle on steep climbs and in sprints
  • Your rider has gestures too, such as putting on a vest
Ride together

Group rides with a six-letter code

Create a room, share the code and ride with up to ten friends. Keep-together holds the group in one bunch, so a stronger rider does not leave the others behind.

FIT files for every app

Every ride saves a standard FIT file. Upload it to Strava, Garmin Connect or any training app.

Ride history

Open a saved ride and see its power zones and heart-rate zones.

ERG and slope

ERG mode holds your watts in a workout. In a free ride, the trainer follows the hills of the road.

FAQ

Questions

Which trainers work?

Smart trainers that use Bluetooth FTMS, Tacx trainers with FE-C over Bluetooth, and Bluetooth heart-rate straps. Your computer needs Bluetooth.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not for workouts and solo rides. Group rides and uploads to Strava or Garmin need a connection. After the alpha, you need internet once to activate your licence.

What will it cost?

The alpha is free. After the alpha we plan one payment per season, with a free trial first. There is no subscription.

Where is my data?

On your computer only: ~/Library/Application Support/Pannolato on macOS, %APPDATA%\Pannolato on Windows. There is no account and no cloud copy.

Which computers are supported?

Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel (macOS 11 or newer) and 64-bit Windows PCs.

The trainer does not connect. What do I do?

Wake the trainer and close other apps that use it, such as Zwift or a phone. Check that Bluetooth is on. See all the steps.

Why do I need an access code?

The alpha is for a small group of testers. Enter your email in the Try the alpha section. We send the code to it. The app asks for the code once.
