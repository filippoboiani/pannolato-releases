Train

Workouts that set the watts for you

Connect a smart trainer and a heart-rate strap over Bluetooth. Pannolato sets the resistance for each step in ERG mode, so you only pedal.

  • A library of workouts: endurance, tempo, sweet spot, threshold, VO2max, sprints
  • Build your own workout, or upload one as JSON
  • A heart-rate cap warns you when you ride too hard
  • Change the target by ±5 W from the keyboard at any time
Ride

Everything you need on one screen

Power, heart rate, cadence and speed sit at the top. The next steps, the climb ahead and your laps sit on the sides. A small coach cheers you on.

  • Speed comes from your power and the gradient of the road
  • The trainer makes the hills feel like hills
  • Switch between rider view and a top view of the map

Ride together

FAQ

Questions

Which trainers work?

Smart trainers that use Bluetooth FTMS, and Bluetooth heart-rate straps. Your computer needs Bluetooth.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not for workouts and solo rides. Group rides and uploads to Strava or Garmin need a connection.

What will it cost?

The alpha is free. After the alpha we plan one payment per season, with a free trial first. There is no subscription.

Where is my data?

On your computer only: ~/Library/Application Support/Pannolato on macOS, %APPDATA%\Pannolato on Windows. There is no account and no cloud copy.

Which computers are supported?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and 64-bit Windows PCs.

Why do I need an access code?

The alpha is for a small group of testers. Ask the team for a code. The app asks for it once.
