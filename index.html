Ride your trainer through a little world

Pannolato drives your smart trainer through structured workouts while you ride a 40 km circuit of arcaded streets, vineyards, desert, coast and autumn forest.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

Train

Workouts that set the watts for you

Connect a smart trainer and a heart-rate strap over Bluetooth. Pannolato sets the resistance for each step in ERG mode, so you only pedal.

  • A library of workouts: endurance, tempo, sweet spot, threshold, VO2max, sprints
  • Build your own workout, or upload one as JSON
  • A heart-rate cap warns you when you ride too hard
  • Change the target by ±5 W from the keyboard at any time
Ride

Everything you need on one screen

Power, heart rate, cadence and speed sit at the top. The next steps, the climb ahead and your laps sit on the sides. A small coach cheers you on.

  • Speed comes from your power and the gradient of the road
  • The trainer makes the hills feel like hills
  • Switch between rider view and a top view of the map
World

A 40 km loop, from Bologna to the forest

Start under the arcades of the city, climb to San Luca, then cross farmland, desert, hills and lakes.

See where you have been

The World map shows the whole circuit, its areas, villages, lakes and rivers, and how far you have ridden so far.

Ride together

Group rides with a six-letter code

Create a room, share the code and ride with up to ten friends. Keep-together holds the group in one bunch, so a stronger rider does not leave the others behind.

Strava and Garmin

Every ride saves a FIT file. Send it to Strava or Garmin Connect with one click.

Training plans

Copy the prompt into ChatGPT, describe your goal, and import the plan it writes.

Your data stays local

Rides and settings live on your computer, in the app's own folder.
