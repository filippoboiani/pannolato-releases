Pannolato

Privacy policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Pannolato is an indoor cycling app for macOS and Windows. We collect as little personal data as we can. Your rides, workouts and settings stay on your computer. This policy tells you which data we process, why, and for how long.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Filippo Boiani, VAT number (P.IVA) 03761021207.

Contact for all privacy questions: pannolato.indoor@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your computer

The app stores your rides, workouts, training plans, settings and the access code or licence key in its folder on your computer:

  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Pannolato
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Pannolato

We do not receive this data. The app has no account, no analytics and no tracking. To delete this data, delete the folder.

If you connect Strava, the app sends the rides you choose from your computer directly to Strava, with the Strava API keys you enter. The Garmin button opens the Garmin Connect import page, where you upload the file yourself. Strava and Garmin process this data under their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies. It loads the Cloudflare Web Analytics script, which counts page views without cookies and without an identifier on your device. It saves your language choice in your browser's local storage, only when you choose a language. The app stores its data on your computer, as section 2 explains.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to pannolato.indoor@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

Most of the data in section 3 is deleted within a day or is never stored, so often there is nothing for us to give or delete.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

8. Children

Pannolato is not for children under 14. We do not knowingly process their data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version on this page with a new effective date. For important changes, we also tell you in the app.
